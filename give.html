Why We Give

Every Gift Awakens a Life

"But this I say, He which soweth sparingly shall reap also sparingly; and he which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully. Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver." — 2 Corinthians 9:6–7

Kindly contact the church office for our official giving account details.

Ways to Give

Choose How You Want to Sow

Your giving supports worship, the Word, missions and community care.

In-Person During Service

Place your offering or tithe in the offering basket during any of our services, or simply ask any usher for assistance.

Bank Transfer

Reach out to the church office for our official bank account name and number. Please use your name and the offering type as the narration.

Mobile Money / USSD

We accept gifts via bank transfer and select mobile money options. Contact us for the current channels and instructions.

Give in Kind

Items for missions, outreach, children's ministry and welfare drives are always appreciated. Speak to the church office to arrange drop-off.

Thank You

We are Grateful

Your generosity makes it possible to awaken more lives to the abundant life in Christ — in Port Harcourt and beyond. Thank you for sowing into the kingdom.
