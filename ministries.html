There Is a Place for You

Serve, Grow, and Belong

Every believer has a gift and a place in the body of Christ. Find yours.

Worship & Music

The worship team leads the church into God's presence through instruments, vocals and media each service.

Word & Bible Study

Teachers and leaders who make the Word practical, awakening understanding and faith in every believer.

Men's & Women's Fellowship

Dedicated gatherings where men and women grow in godliness, purpose and mentorship together.

Children & Youth Ministry

Age-appropriate teaching and fun that shape the next generation of world-changers for God's kingdom.

Prayer & Intercession

Intercessors who stand in the gap through fervent corporate and personal prayer for breakthrough.

Outreach & Evangelism

Taking the message of abundant life beyond our walls — to the streets, schools, hospitals and nations.

Hospitality & Ushering

A warm welcome for every guest — first-timers and returning friends alike feel at home at Awaken Life.

Media & Technology

Sound, visuals and online broadcast that carry the awakening Gospel to those who cannot be with us.

Welfare & Community Care

Practical support for widows, families and those in need — the love of Christ shown in action.

Ready to Serve?

Discover Your Place

Whether God is calling you to lead worship, teach the Word, pray for others or welcome strangers, there is a place for you in the body of Christ at Awaken Life.
