Our Mandate

Awakening Lives to Abundant Life

Awaken Life International Ministry is a ministry raised in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, with a divine mandate: to awaken men and women to the abundant life that is in Christ Jesus (John 10:10).

We are committed to declaring the fullness of life that Christ purchased — a life of grace, purpose, wholeness and joy. From sleep to sonship, we walk with every believer in the awakening of their true identity in Jesus.

God has been faithful in and through this ministry. We are a family of faith where the Word is preached with clarity, worship flows freely, and every person is nurtured toward wakefulness and wholeness in Christ.
