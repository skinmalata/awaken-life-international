Our Mandate

Awakening Lives to Abundant Life

Awaken Life International Ministry is a family of believers raised in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, with a divine mandate: to awaken men and women to the abundant life that is in Christ Jesus (John 10:10).

We are a vibrant, spirit-filled church where the Word is preached with clarity, worship flows freely, and every soul is nurtured in the grace and purpose of God. Here, lives are awakened — from sleep to sonship, from striving to rest, from fear to fullness.

  • ✦ Christ-centred preaching & teaching
  • ✦ An atmosphere charged with God's presence
  • ✦ A warm, family-like community
  • ✦ Prayer, healing & breakthrough
